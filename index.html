How it works

Every scan cross-references live Kalshi prices against outside reference points — Polymarket, sportsbook moneylines and player props, and years of Kalshi's own settled-market history — to estimate a true win probability for each market, then flags the ones where Kalshi's price and that estimate disagree by a meaningful margin. Only markets clearing both a minimum confidence level and a minimum price edge get surfaced.
